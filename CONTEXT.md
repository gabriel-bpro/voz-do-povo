# Context

Product language is pt-BR; repo and code language is English.

## Glossary

- **Federal politician** — a person holding (or elected to) a federal mandate in Brazil: deputado federal, senador, or presidente. The only people the MVP covers.
- **Diligence quartet** — the four MVP datasets about a politician: expenses, votes/attendance, declared patrimony (TSE), campaign financing (TSE).
- **Home dashboard** — the app's landing surface: national rankings and highlights (e.g. top expenses), not a single-politician page.
- **Politician profile** — the per-politician page assembling the diligence quartet in plain language.
- **Plain-language report** — any rendered explanation whose numbers come from structured, source-traceable data (templates). In the MVP the prose is templated too (no LLM prose).
- **NL query** — _(dropped from the MVP, pivot of 2026-09-02)_ a former natural-language question feature; the LLM never answers free-form questions in the MVP.
- **Plain-language walkthrough** — _(dropped from the MVP, second pivot of 2026-09-02)_ formerly the LLM's job of narrating a page's data; the MVP has no LLM prose at all — plain language on the site comes from **templates** only.
- **Follow your politician** — _(post-MVP, designed 2026-09-02)_ subscription on a politician profile; citizens receive e-mail notifications when their data changes, the e-mail body generated by the LLM (one call per change event, fanned out to all followers of that politician). The LLM's first and only job post-MVP.
- **Mirror** — our own copy of government open data, synced per source cadence; the app never calls gov APIs at request time.
- **Emenda parlamentar** — a budget amendment authored by a parliamentarian, directing federal money to a destination (municipality, state, entity). Tracked via Portal da Transparência: value committed (empenhado) vs paid (pago).
- **Emenda PIX** — an emenda de transferência especial (RP6): federal money transferred directly to a state/municipal fund without a specific destined object, the hardest kind of emenda to trace. Always flagged distinctly wherever emendas are shown.
- **Attention badge** ("chama atenção") — a marker on an expense or emenda that trips one of a small fixed set of transparent rules (expense above the category median, counterparty in CEIS/CNEP, emenda PIX concentration). Rules are fixed and explainable, never ML-generated; the badge shows the bare underlying number (median, percentage, list membership + sync date) as neutral context, never an editorialized judgment.
- **Canonical politician** — our single internal entity for a real-world federal politician, linked to per-source identifiers (Câmara URI id, Senado code, TSE sequencial/CPF/título eleitoral) via the source-id map. All cross-source joins go through it.
- **Raw layer** — staging tables where syncs land source data verbatim, with provenance (source, pulled-at). Never read by the app.
- **Curated layer** — cleaned, unified per-dataset tables (one `expense` table across houses, etc.) promoted from the raw layer; the only schema the app queries.
